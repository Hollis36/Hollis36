# Peifu Zhang

I work on **multimodal perception**, **industrial embodied intelligence**, **robot learning**, and **AI systems for smart manufacturing**. My research focuses on robust visual sensing, RGB-T / RGB-D fusion, UAV and anti-UAV perception, vision-language-action models, and deployable AI workflows for scientific and industrial scenarios.

I am trained in artificial intelligence, biomedical engineering, and mechanical engineering, with current research interests spanning intelligent integrated manufacturing, multimodal physical information fusion, industrial inspection, robotics, and AI for Science.

## Research Interests

- Multimodal perception and sensor fusion
- RGB-T / infrared-visible object detection
- UAV and anti-UAV intelligent sensing
- Vision-language and vision-language-action models
- Robot learning and embodied intelligence
- Smart manufacturing, industrial inspection, and digital twins
- Physics-informed learning and AI for scientific / engineering systems
- Optical materials, NIR-II sensing, and AI-assisted characterization

- [**newton-vla-demo**](https://github.com/Hollis36/newton-vla-demo) — a 3-minute classroom demo of embodied AI on a laptop: Newton physics + an LLM as the VLA brain
